# My Church

**[Victory Baptist Church](https://victorybaptistolathe.org/)**

- Address: [15075 W. 143rd St., Olathe, Kansas](https://maps.app.goo.gl/t5ArzeahM28pUgea7)
- Sundays: 
    - 10:00 AM - Sunday School
    - 11:00 AM - Sunday Service
    - 6:00 PM - Sunday Evening Service
- Wednesdays:
    - 7:00 PM - Midweek Service

Victory Baptist Church of Olathe is a traditional, conservative local church that has been in the community for over 40 years. The pastor, Gary Dackin, is a very kind, genuine, and faithful man. We are praying and laboring to see if God will revive our church and lead us into another season of growing in the Lord Jesus Christ through evangelizing the lost (the preaching of the gospel) and edifying the saved (the preaching, teaching, and application of Scripture). 

If that is something that interests you, please visit! We would love to meet you, hear your story, and see what God can do with us together. 

If you would like to see what the services are like, you can visit: 
- The church's [website](https://victorybaptistolathe.org/)
- The church's [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100064714657386) page
- The church's [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@VictoryBaptistOlathe) channel.
